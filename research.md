# Research

My research advances computer vision through dataset-driven discovery of new problem spaces, with a focus on accessibility and visual privacy for blind and low-vision people.

## Themes
- **Vision-language evaluation in the real world:** benchmarking and failure analysis
- **Dataset curation:** creating datasets that reveal under-studied challenges
- **Visual privacy:** identifying and managing private content in images
- **Few-shot learning & segmentation:** robust learning with limited data

## Selected projects (sample)
- **BIV-Priv-Seg:** locating private content in images taken by people with visual impairments  
- **VizWiz-FewShot:** few-shot object localization for assistive vision tasks
